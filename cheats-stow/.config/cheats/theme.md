# Look

Gruvbox everywhere, one bar (outline). Nothing to switch except wallpaper.

| How | What |
|---|---|
| `Super+Alt+W` | wallpaper cards |
| `~/.config/waybar/scripts/wallpaper.sh random` | random wallpaper |

Wallpapers: `~/realm/builds/dotfiles-acer/wallpapers/`, active one is the `current` symlink (hyprpaper + hyprlock use it).

Colours: `themes/gruvbox.*` in kitty, hypr, mako, rofi; `waybar/style.css`; `gtk-{3,4}.0/gtk.css`; `starship/starship.toml`; `zsh/theme.zsh`; `lazygit/config.yml`; `yazi/theme.toml`; `btop/btop.conf.in`; `nvim/lua/plugins/theme.lua`.
